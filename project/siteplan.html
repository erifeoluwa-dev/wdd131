DM4Autos Site Plan

Site Name

DM4Autos — an abbreviation drawn from the names of my intended children, representing a personal and lasting connection to the business.

It also doubles as "Direct Message for Autos," reflecting a car stand built on direct, personal communication with buyers.

Site Purpose

The site gives visitors a warm welcome and communicates the meaning behind the DM4Autos brand. It provides information and tips for learning about cars, and creates a platform for clients to better understand the car marketplace before making a purchase decision.

Scenarios

  1. What common problems or issues does this car model have?
  2. Was this car involved in any accidents?
  3. What is the current condition of the car?
  4. How do I know if I'm getting a fair price for this car?

Color Schema

Black (#000000)
Used for header/footer backgrounds, body text, and the overall page background.
Gold (#D4AF37)
Used for headings, logo, buttons, hover states, and accent borders.

Typography

Cinzel
Used for headings and the site title, giving the brand an elegant, prestigious tone that matches the gold shield emblem in the logo.
Montserrat
Used for body text, navigation, and general content, providing clean readability that balances Cinzel's formal character.
